San Antonio Housing Market: The September 2026 Numbers, Explained

Every month we break down the San Antonio housing numbers so you don't have to dig through reports. Here's September 2026.

The numbers (San Antonio metro, September 2026)

  • Homes sold: 2,793, down about 1% from a year ago
  • Median price: $302,999, down about 2%
  • Average price: $363,341, down about 2%
  • Days on market: 88, up 11%
  • Months of inventory: 5.95
  • Active listings: 17,155, up 4%
  • New listings: 4,172, up 3%
  • Pending sales: 2,091, down 16%
  • Sold at: 92.4% of original list price
  • Average rent: $1,831

Bexar County and Texas

  • Bexar County single-family sales: 1,839, up 6%
  • Bexar County median: $282,680, down 4.8%
  • Bexar County months of inventory: 5.4
  • Texas statewide median: $330,000, down 0.7%

What it means if you're buying

  • More choice: inventory is up and homes sit longer
  • More leverage: ask for closing-cost help or repairs
  • Insurance: Texas homeowners insurance filings since July averaged a 4.3% decrease

What it means if you're selling

  • Price right: homes that start too high are the ones that sit
  • Watch pending sales: down 16%, so buyers are more careful
  • Prepare: repairs, photos and easy showings win in a slower market

Questions people ask

Is San Antonio a buyer's market right now?

Conditions lean toward buyers more than a year ago: 5.95 months of inventory, 88 days on market and homes selling for 92.4% of original list price in September 2026.

What is the median home price in San Antonio?

$302,999 for the San Antonio metro in September 2026, according to SABOR.
