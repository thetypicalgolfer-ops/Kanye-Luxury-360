10 Red Flags Your San Antonio Realtor Is Quietly Costing You Money

Choosing the wrong agent is the single most expensive mistake a home seller can make — and it almost never shows up until the closing table, when the money is already gone. Most sellers never see it coming because the warning signs are quiet.

Here are ten red flags to watch for before you sign a listing agreement anywhere in San Antonio or the Texas Hill Country. If your agent trips several of these, keep interviewing.

1. They priced your home without a written CMA

A number pulled from thin air — or one inflated just to win your listing — is not a strategy. Insist on a written comparative market analysis with recent, local comps you can actually see.

2. They want to represent both sides of the deal

Double-ending a transaction can quietly work against your price. When one agent serves both buyer and seller, ask exactly whose interest is being protected.

3. There's no written marketing plan

“I'll put it on the MLS” is not marketing a luxury home. You want photography, video, targeted digital ads, and an off-market network — in writing.

4. They sell real estate part-time

An agent who closed two homes last year is learning on your listing. In a market this specific, you want someone in it every single day.

5. Zero budget for professional media

Luxury buyers judge in the first photo. If your agent shoots listings on a phone, your home is already losing to the one down the street.

6. They dodge questions about commission or contract terms

Transparency now predicts transparency later. An agent who won't clearly explain their fee and their agreement will not get clearer under pressure.

7. They don't know your local comps

A Hill Country acreage estate is not priced like a suburban tract home. If your agent can't speak fluently about your specific corridor, they'll misprice it.

8. They “bought the listing” with the highest number

The agent who promises the biggest price isn't the best — they're often just telling you what you want to hear so you'll sign. The market decides, not the pitch.

9. They're slow to respond during the courtship

If communication is spotty while they're trying to win you, it disappears once you're under contract. Watch how fast they answer now.

10. No verifiable recent sales or references

A slick presentation is not a track record. Ask for recent, comparable homes they've actually sold — and call the references.

An honest agent will hand you the truth even when it isn't what you hoped to hear — because the truth is what gets your home sold for the most money, in the least time, with the fewest surprises. That's the standard at Kanye Concierge 360.
